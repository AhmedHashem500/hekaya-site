In one sentence: this is a private tool for one person to publish their own work to their own accounts; it is not a product, not a service, and is offered to nobody.

Scope

HEKAYA Publisher is software written and used by its author to upload films the author made to accounts the author owns. It is not sold, licensed, hosted or made available to third parties, and there is no sign-up.

How it behaves

  • Nothing is uploaded without a human approving that specific item first.
  • Only original work produced by HEKAYA is published. No third-party media is reused.
  • Platform rules are checked by the tool before an upload is offered for approval, and a failing check blocks it.
  • No attempt is made to evade any platform's limits, detection or review.

Platform terms come first

Use of the tool is subject to the terms of each platform it connects to, including the YouTube Terms of Service, the YouTube API Services Terms of Service, and Meta's platform terms. Where those disagree with anything here, they win.
