HEKAYA حكاية

Short illustrated true stories, in Arabic and English.

HEKAYA is an independent, one-person studio. Each story is researched from documented history, written, illustrated as original vector artwork, narrated, and published as a short vertical film in two languages.

The publisher

This page exists because HEKAYA uses a small private tool — the HEKAYA Publisher — to upload its own finished films to its own accounts on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.

The tool runs entirely on one personal computer. It has no website, no users other than its author, no servers, and no database anywhere but that machine. It uploads a film only after a human has reviewed it and pressed a button, every single time.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process anyone else's data.
  • It does not offer accounts, sign-in or any service to other people.
  • It does not read, scrape or republish anyone else's content.
  • It does not post automatically. Every upload is approved by a person first.
